Jak and Daxter 2 is probably one of the most nostalgic games on the PS2 for me. I only remember beating it one time on the PS2, and I vividly remember how difficult some of the missions were, especially the little side missions that Torn gives you. This entry in the Jak series introduced guns, and cut back on the amount of checkpoints in each mission. A bunch of missions are pretty much one long sprint, and it definitely made me angry at some points. I recently replayed it via OpenGOAL and it was SUCH a blast, 1440p, 240fps, 16 anisotropic filtering, it was such a great time, aside from some painful missions. I had to temporarily restore the game back to 60fps for one specific missions, where you have to babysit some goons to blow up the statue of Mar in the sewers, that was annoying. Tough mission too.
