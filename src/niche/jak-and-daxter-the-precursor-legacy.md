Jak I for the Playstation 2 is by far one of my favorite games of all time. I only recall finishing it one time on the PS2, where I also collected all 101 Power Cells, the reward for doing so was... pretty boring, but was still a blast. I recently replayed the game with my friend Ada... in multiplayer! There is a client called TeamRuns that allows for playing with friends, with very little issues. Only a few desyncs overall, mainly with boss phases. Our lobby was password protected with the password "test" and someone got in, but thankfully they just wanted to play the game. Will definitely try it out again.
