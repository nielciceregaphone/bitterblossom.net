# Lego Automata

I have always been very critical/picky about my [Lego MOCs](https://rebrickable.com/help/what-is-a-lego-moc/). It has been years of collecting, scavenging bulk bins, and coming up with something that works well for me. One of my absolute favorite things to build with Lego are mechs. Mechas, automatons, drones, servos, you name it. In this post I will be showing my recent creations, as  well as what lore/ideas have gone into them.

## Ongoing projects

This is Laravel, it is currently just a frame, but it is quite large, and very posable.

![Laravel's body](https://images.spellbound.sh/16235647-dd40-47d7-b300-e24d934f3ef0.jpg)

Here is a shot of just the torso frame. The waist design was shamelessly yoinked by [RansomFern](https://rebrickable.com/users/Ransom_Fern/mocs/).

![Laravel pose 1](https://images.spellbound.sh/cfe489b3-9cfd-4a11-a2a9-a530e7676ccd.jpg)

Here is one of the poses, Laravel has quite a lot of articulation, but the leg assembly is a little jank. Still looks cool though!!!

![Laravel pose 2](https://images.spellbound.sh/IMG_20260625_235553249_HDR.jpg)

Here is the second pose for Laravel. 

Laravel is still at this state, and will likely be some time before I attempt to add armor to the frame. I do quite like how it looks even without armor, but if I had to choose a color, it would likely be silver.

## Finished projects

## Mini Automata (cont. Finished projects)

![Trio](https://images.spellbound.sh/IMG_20260805_201911_209.jpg)

From left to right, we have:

Jab, Scope, and Queen.

In addition to this trio, here is Solo:

![Solo](https://images.spellbound.sh/IMG_20260806_165900_887.jpg)

These four mechs are all apart of the same class. They are all of the same power and capability.

![Jab](https://images.spellbound.sh/IMG_20260805_201105_392.jpg)

Jab is a yellow mech, featuring child-bearing hips, which allow for extremely powerful thrust, which can be combined with their *jabbing* fists.

![Scope](https://images.spellbound.sh/IMG_20260805_201717_803.jpg)

Scope is green with a massive ego. Their right arm can be reconfigured between being a shotgun or a long range sniper.

![Queen](https://images.spellbound.sh/IMG_20260805_201301_046.jpg)

Queen is the blue "summoner" of the bunch, featuring two tiny pistols, as well as two servos, Cyrus & Cymbal.

![Solo](https://images.spellbound.sh/IMG_20260806_165923_865.jpg)

Lastly there is Solo. A white mech that is missing their left arm. To compensate, Solo had opted to have two arms on the right side of their torso. Two heavy duty jabbing weapons, similar to Jab's.
